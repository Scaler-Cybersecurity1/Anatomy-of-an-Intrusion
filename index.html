    Anatomy of an Intrusion

    A finance workstation, WKS-FIN-07, was compromised last week. Forensics pulled 8 artifacts off it — a document, process events, registry keys, network beacons, a cleared log. Your job is to map each one to the attacker's tactic, then order them into the story of the attack.

    How this lab works

    1. Map the column: for each artifact, choose the MITRE ATT&CK tactic it represents from the dropdown (Initial Access, Execution, Persistence, Credential Access, Discovery, Defense Evasion, Command & Control, Exfiltration).
    2. One tactic in the list is a decoy — not every option is used, and no tactic repeats.
    3. Get all 8 right to unlock the next step: order the artifacts into the sequence the attack actually unfolded.
    4. Finally, recover three facts from the artifacts to build the flag. Submit it on your course platform.
    5. Everything is checked in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere. Progress is saved on this device.
